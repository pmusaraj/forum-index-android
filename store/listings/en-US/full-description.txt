Discover conversations across Discourse communities with Forum Index.

Browse recent discussions in the Trending feed, choose the subjects you want to follow, and arrange your subject tabs. Open topics in the app, share a discussion, or save it to your bookmarks for later.

Features:
• Browse discussions across multiple forums.
• Customize and reorder your subject tabs.
• Save bookmarked topics on your device.
• Read topics in the built-in viewer or open them externally.
• Unlock For You recommendations through reading, bookmarking, and topic reports, with persistent opt-out in Settings.

Your device automatically enrolls with a persistent friendly nickname. You can opt out in Settings and continue browsing; saved bookmarks stay on your device. Generate a new nickname in Settings and choose Auto, Light, or Dark appearance. An internet connection is required to load feeds and forum pages.
