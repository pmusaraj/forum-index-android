Discover conversations across Discourse communities with Forum Index.

Browse recent discussions in the main feed, choose the subjects you want to follow, and arrange your subject tabs. Open topics in the app, share a discussion, or save it to your starred list for later.

Features:
• Browse discussions across multiple forums.
• Customize and reorder your subject tabs.
• Save starred topics on your device.
• Read topics in the built-in viewer or open them externally.
• Optionally enable contributions to personalize your main feed and help improve the index through reading, starring, and topic reports.

You can browse without enrolling as a contributor. Contributions are optional and can be turned off in the app. An internet connection is required to load feeds and forum pages.
